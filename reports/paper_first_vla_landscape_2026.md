# Paper-First VLA Method Landscape 2026

Date: 2026-07-11 KST

Branch: `codex/paper-first-vla-ral-method-design`

## Review Standard

This review prioritizes method-level novelty over benchmark mining. The target contribution must not be LoRA, SmolVLA, OpenVLA, quantization, generic confidence, generic verification, generic progress prediction, generic replanning, generic chunking, adapter routing, or a benchmark-only observation.

Primary-source count reviewed: `34`.

Primary sources were verified from arXiv, official project pages, official repositories, or official technical reports. Dates below are arXiv submission dates unless an official report page is cited.

## Mechanism Map

| Mechanism family | Close papers | Current center of gravity | Implicit open pressure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General VLA action generation | RT-1, RT-2, Open X-Embodiment/RT-X, Octo, OpenVLA, pi0, OpenVLA-OFT, SmolVLA, GR00T N1, Gemini Robotics | scale, pretraining, action tokenization, flow/continuous heads, action chunking, asynchronous execution | action learning is still mostly optimized by token likelihood, regression, or flow loss rather than downstream closed-loop effect |
| Action chunking and real-time execution | SmolVLA, OpenVLA-OFT, FASTER, AAC, Legato, DREAM-Chunk, VLA-Corrector, SEAM | lower latency, smoother chunk boundaries, event-triggered correction, reactive horizon selection | these methods decide when or how to execute chunks, but generally do not assign causal closed-loop credit to the action effect each chunk produced |
| Verification, confidence, and pre-execution selection | VLAConf, CoVer/CoVer-VLA, VeriSpace, Pre-VLA | confidence heads, contrastive instruction-action verifiers, 3D-aware action verification, safety/advantage heads | selection quality is modeled as confidence or validity, not as the estimated interventional contribution of a chunk to required task-state predicates |
| Progress and recovery | SPR, ProgressVLA, ProgVLA, REMAC | milestone progress, progress-guided diffusion, progress-aware critics, reflection and rewind | progress is treated as a scalar or planned milestone order; contact and object-state effects can be nonmonotonic and phase-dependent |
| Robustness and partial observability | LIBERO-Plus, LIBERO-Occ/VIM, STRONG-VLA, CRT, LIBERO-CF/CAG | perturbation benchmarks, occlusion completion, corruption restoration, language shortcut mitigation | robustness fixes are mostly input-side or selection-side; action-effect credit under deployment shift remains under-modeled |
| Adaptation and prior retention | PriorVLA, CLARE, VLA-GSE, OpenVLA-OFT | prior experts, autonomous adapters, spectral expert decomposition, optimized fine-tuning | adaptation preserves or routes priors, but does not directly solve objective-evaluation mismatch between action loss and closed-loop success |
| World-model interaction | Gemini Robotics-ER, ProgressVLA, Pre-VLA, DREAM-Chunk | embodied reasoning, latent future states, world-model rollout screening, candidate latent futures | world models are usually used to predict future states for verification or progress, not to train an effect-credit objective for VLA action learning |

## Landscape Conclusion

The newest literature is dense around test-time confidence, verification, progress, correction, chunking, robustness perturbations, and adapter/prior preservation. A viable RA-L method should therefore move one level deeper: it should change how VLA policies assign training credit to action chunks based on their causal effect on task-relevant physical predicates. That gap is not merely stated by one paper; it is implied by the shared reliance on action losses, progress scalars, confidence scores, or candidate validity proxies while final evaluation remains closed-loop task success.
