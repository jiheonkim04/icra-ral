# Epoch 7 RA-L Venue Rules Audit

Audit date: 2026-07-20 (Asia/Seoul)

Primary authority: IEEE Robotics and Automation Society, [RA-L Information for Authors](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ra-l/ra-l-information-for-authors/).

## Current constraints

- Scope: timely, concise, innovative robotics and automation research ideas or application results.
- Initial paper: two-column IEEE conference format using `ieeeconf`, US Letter, 10 pt.
- Length: six pages including figures and references; at most two charged extra pages; absolute maximum eight pages. Appendices count toward the limit. Supplemental text or figures may not be hidden in multimedia.
- Review: double anonymous. The initial manuscript must leave byline space but omit author identities.
- Keywords: two to five; the first two must come from the RA-L controlled keyword set; at most two may be free-form.
- PDF: minimum accepted PDF version is 1.4; margins and embedded fonts must pass PaperPlaza checks.
- Multimedia: one video maximum. Code, data, readme, and instruction files are permitted. All multimedia must be submitted as one ZIP no larger than 50 MB, with `ReadMe.txt` and `Summary.txt`. The paper must remain self-contained.
- Submission is year-round through PaperPlaza. This audit authorizes no submission or external upload.

## Epoch 7 packaging consequences

Any paper package must target six pages first and treat pages seven and eight as an explicit cost-bearing contingency. Evidence, equations, ablations, limitations, and reproducibility facts needed to understand the claim must remain in the paper. The initial source must be anonymous and use the official conference-style class. A video may illustrate closed-loop behavior but cannot carry missing scientific argumentation.

## Source excerpts verified

The official page states the six-page/two-extra-page rule, double-anonymous review, `ieeeconf` initial format, byline anonymity, two-to-five keyword rule, PDF 1.4 minimum, one-video rule, and 50 MB multimedia archive limit. These facts were checked directly on 2026-07-20 and should be rechecked immediately before any future submission.
