# Researcher Rebuttal: DICD-VLA

Date: 2026-07-12 KST
Role: Researcher A

Reviewer B is correct that DEFLECT and TIC-VLA create serious novelty pressure.

The first prototype will therefore avoid broad claims. It will test only this local hypothesis:

`A small explicit delay-indexed adapter over frozen SmolVLA action chunks can outperform direct chunk-index execution under controlled LIBERO action delay.`

The prototype will not claim:

- official DEFLECT reproduction;
- general delay-robust VLA post-training;
- asynchronous real-robot deployment;
- backbone-independent success;
- RA-L readiness from SmolVLA alone.

Researcher A accepts direct chunk-index execution as the simple killer baseline. If DICD does not beat it with a valid active mechanism, the method is killed or archived without rescue.
