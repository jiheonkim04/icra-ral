# Epoch 8 Mandated Rotation Ranking

Decision time: 2026-07-20T20:28:28+09:00

The exact PCAT language mechanism is archived after a valid frozen Stage-0
failure. Its direct reparameterizations are not eligible. The alternate
two-sided binding-posterior mechanism was rejected before outcomes because
the current literature and local implementation boundary are occupied. The
verified problem--instruction sensitivity and target binding in otherwise
competent VLA behavior--is preserved as evidence, but no third language
mechanism is promoted without a distinct current novelty boundary.

## Why rank 1 is a legal reopening

Epoch 7 closed only the *standard-demonstration replay oracle*: the selected
replay succeeded under the altered drawer and bowl conditions but failed the
altered plate and button conditions despite target contact. Its own closure
explicitly did not establish task impossibility. Epoch 8's rotation contract
permits an altered-dynamics controller or new expert that is valid under the
altered dynamics, and forbids treating ordinary demonstration replay as the
only feasibility proof. A state-feedback expert is therefore a materially new
protocol, not a relabeling or hyperparameter rescue of a policy result; no VLA
dynamics outcome has yet been produced.

The new oracle is limited to feasibility. It may read simulator geometry and
the official success predicate to stop, but it is never a policy comparator,
never counted as VLA success, and never supplies actions or privileged state to
a VLA. Development and validation reset identities are disjoint. Controller
search is frozen before development outcomes, and the selected controller must
be frozen again before any sealed validation outcome is read.

## Current novelty boundary retained from the same-date primary audit

The bounded claim is not that physical robustness is wholly unstudied. Eva-VLA
and LIBERO-PRO perturb visible or semantic task factors; J-PARC perturbs
robot-side joint properties and evaluates task success; LIBERO-CF and
RoboSemanticBench separate language or target grounding. The surviving local
boundary is the joint use of a visually and semantically paired object- or
environment-dynamics intervention, a correct-target-contact endpoint, and
completion conditional on contact. This is an evidence-bounded novelty
inference through 2026-07-20, not a universal absence claim.
