## Novelty decision

`B. INCREMENTAL_BUT_POTENTIALLY_PUBLISHABLE`

The broad idea is not new. Multi-camera teacher-to-single-camera student action and feature distillation was published in RA-L in 2023. Complete sensor/camera dropout training, multi-view shared/private representations, VLA hidden/action distillation, separate arm/gripper losses, action-guided view-invariant representation learning, and cross-viewpoint reconstruction also exist as separate mechanisms.

The remaining defensible contribution is narrower:

> Adapt a frozen multi-view VLA to a verified complete wrist-view failure by distilling matched clean-teacher action-chunk representations and continuous actions into a parameter-efficient dropout student, preserving the raw gripper decision margin separately, while using cross-view wrist reconstruction only as a training regularizer and never as a policy input. Deployment uses no teacher, future frame, retrieval library, expert action, or reconstructed image/token.

This is a conditional contribution, not a current paper claim. It becomes defensible only if the frozen experiment shows a practical mechanism effect beyond both the no-reconstruction ablation and a capacity/data/update-matched generic wrist-dropout adapter, followed by official closed-loop improvement or a preregistered deployment Pareto advantage over `MECHANISM_FAITHFUL_RL4IL_LOCAL_PORT`.

The strongest likely reviewer objection is:

> This is an obvious aggregation of Acar et al.'s multi-to-single-view policy distillation, RME/DisDP sensor-dropout robustness, VITA-VLA/ActDistill action representation distillation, and VILA/MVP-LAM action-centric cross-view objectives. Any gain may be ordinary dropout adaptation rather than the proposed reconstruction or gripper-specific mechanism.

The mandatory generic adaptation control and mechanism ablations are therefore claim-critical, not optional diagnostics.
