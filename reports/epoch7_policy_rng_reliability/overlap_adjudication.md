# Policy-RNG Reliability Overlap Adjudication

Decision: `TOO_OVERLAPPING_OR_TRIVIAL` before empirical execution.

Candidate H asked whether varying only the action-generation seed at fixed LIBERO resets produces a practically important closed-loop outcome distribution. That is not sufficiently distinct from the unresolved Epoch 6 schedule-invariant stochastic VLA route. Epoch 6 already froze the causal chain from request order to process-global RNG assignment, sampled action chunks, physical trajectories, and outcomes. Its valid Stage 0 established action-level schedule dependence; its 40-episode closed-loop test was resource-blocked before any outcome, so the scientific question remains unresolved rather than negatively adjudicated.

Candidate H changes the scheduling intervention into a direct seed factor, but preserves the policy-randomness problem axis, X-VLA/LIBERO Base, ordinary policy inputs, noise-seed intervention, action/success endpoints, and evaluation-reliability archetype. The only residual is a reset-versus-policy variance decomposition, which is standard factorial statistical reporting rather than a new robotics contribution. It therefore supplies only one material change, below the campaign's required two.

The method escape is also occupied. SDN treats the initial diffusion/flow noise as controllable, generates 12 noise-conditioned action candidates per inference step, and selects them using grounding and smoothness scores with closed-loop success gains across multiple VLA families. PhAIL and Beyond Binary Success make distributional outcomes and finite-sample comparison strong controls, not sufficient novelty by themselves.

No policy was loaded, no rollout was run, and no paperability contract was frozen. A serial seed sweep cannot replace the frozen four-shard Epoch 6 intervention; generic variance decomposition, renamed episode-addressed randomness, post-outcome best-seed selection, and an SDN-like candidate selector are prohibited rescues.
