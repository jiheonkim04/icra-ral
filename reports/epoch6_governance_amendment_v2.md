## Authority and mission

The active order is: platform safety and permission rules; the current user
instruction; this amendment; `AGENTS.md`; authoritative Git-tracked state and
raw artifacts; historical reports and retrospectives as evidence only.

The mission is to pursue an honest, reviewer-resistant IEEE RA-L submission
package while a safe, authorized, scientifically defensible action remains.
Positive results and acceptance are never guaranteed. External submission,
publication, public release, paid resources, author contact, and physical-robot
work require explicit user action.

## State machine

The normal terminal state is exactly:

- `RAL_SUBMISSION_PACKAGE_READY_FOR_HUMAN_APPROVAL`

It requires a complete, compiled, audited, double-anonymous RA-L package backed
by positive, valid, claim-aligned empirical evidence. It does not authorize
submission.

The only other terminal states are exactly:

- `HARD_EXTERNAL_BLOCKER_REQUIRES_USER`
- `SAFETY_OR_RESOURCE_STOP_REQUIRES_USER`

`AUTONOMOUS_CAMPAIGN_PAUSED_RESUMABLE` is a resumable operational checkpoint,
not a scientific terminal state. Interruption must preserve durable state and
an exact idempotent resume action. A paper package may be drafted only after
`PAPER_CANDIDATE_GO`; audit failure returns to the relevant repair or empirical
gate and is not terminal.

## Scientific integrity locks

- Scientific truth outranks a preferred story, utilization, speed, or a
  positive outcome.
- Never fabricate or interpolate evidence, invent sources or assets, tune on
  confirmatory outcomes, select favorable tasks/resets/seeds/metrics after the
  fact, hide attempts, or equate offline/replay probes with policy success.
- Discovery, validation, confirmatory, and replication partitions are distinct.
  Historical adaptive evidence remains discovery evidence.
- Every new study freezes its problem, independent unit, manifests, policy
  provenance, success semantics, thresholds, analysis, and missing-key resume
  rule before exposing claim-relevant outcomes.
- A local port is identified as a local port, never an official reproduction.
- Closed-loop policy evidence must execute the stated policy action path in the
  official or explicitly bounded compatible environment.
- Historical non-GO decisions and thresholds are immutable. A new threshold is
  allowed only in a genuinely independent preregistered study and requires a
  prospective semantic, practical-effect, noise, or precision justification.

## Closure locks

The exact scope and one-class classification of historical routes live in
`reports/epoch6_closure_registry.json`. In particular, this campaign does not
reopen wrist-camera dropout or claim-specific asynchronous inference delay.
The TCA/target-conditioned action-heatmap formulation, CSS-Shield, AWF, RIFA,
CVLR, action-consistent missing-view distillation, A2C2 v1, corrected A2C2, and
other audited formulations retain their recorded narrow conclusions. Natural-
reset identity mining remains exhausted. Infrastructure failures are not
scientific disproofs, and formulation closures are not field-wide impossibility
claims.

## Epoch 6 selection and empirical gates

Work proceeds in this order:

`problem -> competent Base -> closest Prior and simple control -> repeated gap
-> recoverable headroom -> thesis -> mechanism -> Stage 0 -> Stage A -> frozen
Stage B -> fresh replication/generalization -> paper-candidate adjudication`

Maintain three to five evidence-backed thesis cards only when defensible,
select one active thesis, and retain at most two ranked backups. Do not name a
method before the problem gate and overlap audit. A valid failure archives only
the tested formulation/causal conclusion and rotates to a scientifically
distinct backup; portfolio exhaustion triggers a current literature/artifact
refresh, not acronym churn or an arbitrary global stopping count.

## Resources and external artifacts

The default Epoch 6 envelope is no paid compute, no physical robot, at most
40 GB of aggregate new downloads, at least 60 GB or 20% free disk (whichever is
larger), at most six configurations in one validation search, at most two
semantically null repair iterations for one non-scientific root cause, and a
checkpoint within every 12 hours of a heavy job. One-backbone residency and the
validated WSL profile are starting points; an actual-path resource smoke must
precede heavy execution. Swap, pagefile growth, CPU/disk offload, or unrelated
user processes may not be hidden as performance strategy.

Every external artifact requires an official source, immutable revision,
provenance, license/use restrictions, size/checksum, remote-code declaration,
and observation/action/normalization/evaluation semantics. Remote code must be
inspected and pinned in an isolated environment.

## Durable operation and handoff

`reports/epoch6_campaign_state.json` is the authoritative resume record. Long
jobs require a durable launch record, PID, heartbeat, progress, stdout/stderr,
atomic partial output, final result/exit code, missing-key-only resume command,
and teardown verification. Never duplicate a live worker or rerun completed
keys to seek a different result.

At coherent checkpoints, update state, evidence and closure ledgers, resource
and provenance manifests, and the compact handoff; validate focused tests and
JSON; run `git diff --check`; inspect scope, secrets, licenses, and large files;
then commit and push the scoped branch if existing credentials and repository
policy permit. Never force-push or commit datasets, weights, credentials, or
large raw media.

## RA-L package policy locks

Before thesis selection and finalization, verify current official RA-L rules.
The current audit records a six-page limit including references, no more than
two paid overlength pages, double-anonymous review, a self-contained multimedia
ZIP of at most 50 MB containing no more than one video, and AI-use disclosure
and human-responsibility requirements. Anonymous paper/media metadata and links
must be audited. Apparent submission-stage conflicts between anonymous-review
rules and AI/multimedia disclosure instructions must be confirmed by a human
with the editor/Papercept before external submission; they are not license to
guess or disclose identity.
