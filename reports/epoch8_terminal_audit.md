## Why no safe decisive local paper path remains

1. The language problem remains real, but the scalar energy and PCAT causal
   mechanisms are closed, while the other audited binding head is directly
   occupied. A third language method would be a new scientific cycle, not a
   missing execution of an already specified method.
2. Semantic-versus-physical attribution still lacks a three-family legal
   feasibility oracle. The newly frozen feedback expert failed even standard
   target contact, so changing its height/orientation/controller after outcomes
   would tune the oracle rather than execute the frozen route.
3. Active hidden-property grounding has a promising narrow Base pattern and
   measurable RGB/proprioceptive mass response, but no complete legal
   probe-return-select controller or trained policy. Both the frozen belief
   mechanism and the frozen scripted return primitive failed. A temporal
   learner or visual-feedback return controller would require new data,
   implementation, splits, and confirmation; it is not a runnable missing
   experiment and cannot inherit the observed validation rows.
4. The two ready concurrency protocols are memory-blocked before outcomes.
   There is no scientifically faithful serial substitution for actual arrival.
5. The same-date literature/artifact and 109-entry route audits found no other
   current local thesis with a distinct untested causal hypothesis, competent
   Base/Prior pair, frozen decisive gate, and paper-capable closed-loop path.
   Those static findings prioritize work; they are not empirical closures.

## Quantitative external resource boundary

The 24,871,014,400-byte host crossed the two-shard 82% ceiling at
21,056,946,176 bytes (84.66%) after two live environments and during model
load, before a forward or outcome. The heartbeat still showed zero WSL swap.
The fully loaded single-environment Stage -1 process peaked at 5,350,780,928
bytes RSS, versus 2,264,317,952 bytes at the interrupted two-shard smoke. Adding
that measured remaining model increment projects 24,143,409,152 bytes used;
keeping it below 82% requires approximately 29.44 decimal GB, making 32 GB the
smallest plausible standard tier for the new two-shard protocol. This is a
projection, so its actual-path resource smoke must still pass.

The archived four-shard study independently projects a 34.96 GB minimum under
its unchanged ceiling; 32 GiB is not certifiable and 48 GB is the smallest
defensible standard tier. A clean 48 GB-or-larger equivalent host is therefore
the one external hardware change that covers every already-frozen concurrency
experiment without changing process/shard semantics. It is not guaranteed to
produce positive science.
