## Strict Salvage Result

Only one family clears the salvage screen:

**Target-Prior TCA family, reframed as Target-Grounded ActionMap / Language-Grounded Action Heatmap.**

The salvage is narrow:

- keep: target prior audits, non-leaking object/instruction grounding, LIBERO-Para metrics, wrong-target metrics, old fixed-prior positive signal;
- discard: TCA-Select, weak 7D MLP TCA heads, local proxy claims, contact-set injection, canonicalization-only PRISM framing;
- require: an ActionMap-style anchor that beats mean action, linear/L1, and simple MLP before any target-grounded extension is treated as method evidence.

## Non-Salvageable Families

Repair, shielding, temporal retiming, temporal-logic repair, augmentation, active probing, safety preferences, and contact-set injection should not be revived as current main RA-L routes. They either lost to a simple baseline, lacked utility preservation, required too much source/compute, or failed before a real VLA/official benchmark path was established.
