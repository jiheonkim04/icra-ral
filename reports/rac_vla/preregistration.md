## Frozen Method Identity

RAC-VLA is a Reflective VLA prior extension. It learns a compact action-consequence calibration context and uses it to gate a bounded residual calibration around frozen SmolVLA actions.

No full-model VLA fine-tuning is allowed in this cycle.

- `AUDIT_STOP_DATA_FAILURE`;
- `AUDIT_STOP_NO_HEADROOM`;

- `AUDIT_STOP_DESIGN_FAILURE`.

Do not proceed to rollout if Stage 0 stops.

Synthetic labels are generated by constructing command actions `S_k^{-1}(a)` from clean trace actions `a`, not by directly treating perturbed trace actions as the clean command.

## Validation Search

Maximum six configurations:

- history horizon `H in {2, 4}`;
- residual coefficient `alpha in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20}`.

Validation score:

`score = 0.35 * full_vs_best_baseline_margin + 0.25 * shifted_proxy_gain + 0.20 * clean_retention + 0.15 * mechanism_activation + 0.05 * action_validity`

All components are computed on validation identities only. Offline action L2 alone cannot select the final configuration.

## Stage A

Use approximately `10` paired episodes per policy.

Policies:

1. `base_smolvla_shifted`
2. `reflective_history_proxy`
3. `rac_full`
4. `rac_no_consequence_ablation`
5. `online_diagonal_inverse_gain`

Stage A may permanently kill only under current governance:

- mechanism invalidity;
- no headroom;
- catastrophic degradation;
- exact trivial equivalence;
- clear baseline or ablation dominance satisfying Stage A kill rules.

Otherwise advance to Stage B.

## Stage B

Use at least `40` paired episodes per key policy on the identical task/reset manifest.

Report:

- successes/counts;
- task-balanced success;
- paired wins/losses/ties;
- paired bootstrap confidence interval;
- failure-rate reduction;
- mechanism activation;
- clean retention;
- latency and VRAM where available.

## Prototype GO

RAC reaches prototype GO only if:

- `rac_full` beats Base, Reflective proxy, no-consequence ablation, and online inverse-gain;
- absolute task-balanced gain is at least `10` points at prototype scale, or paired evidence is consistently positive with meaningful failure-rate reduction;
- mechanism activation is nonzero and bounded;
- no privileged inference signal is used;
- clean behavior is retained.

## Kill

Permanent kill when Stage B is valid and complete and any holds:

- online inverse-gain or Reflective proxy matches or beats RAC;
- no-consequence ablation matches or beats RAC;
- RAC is clearly worse than Base;
- paired upper confidence bound excludes useful improvement;
- clean retention fails.

No third expansion after the one allowed Stage B expansion to at most `80` paired episodes per key policy.
