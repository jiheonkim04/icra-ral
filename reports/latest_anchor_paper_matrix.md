# Latest Anchor Paper Matrix

Literature-first scan date: 2026-07-08. This matrix is for topic selection only. No implementation or experiment is proposed here.

| Anchor | Source | What it advances | What it still does not settle | Topic-selection implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct Action-Head Injection of a Grounded 3D Point, 2026-06 | https://arxiv.org/html/2606.27663v1 | Shows large LIBERO-PRO gains from directly injecting one grounded 3D point into VLA action heads. | Does not prove that richer contact sets beat the active single-point baseline; local ContactSet-VLA already failed that gate. | Reject contact-set follow-up unless a new literature gap avoids single-point/destination-only dominance. |
| VLA-FAIL, 2026-06 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21386 | Early task-failure detection for finetuned VLAs using internal feature deviation and action-plan consistency. | Detection is not recovery; robustness against no-repair, safety-only, and abstain-only policies must be tested. | Candidate area: failure detection plus evidence-calibrated stop/retry only if it beats no-repair and safety-only on replay/control. |
| Decoupling Declarative from Procedural in VLA, 2026-06 | https://arxiv.org/html/2606.21496v1 | Targets the entanglement between what the instruction declares and how action procedures unfold. | Must show gains beyond canonicalization and simple held-out paraphrase baselines. | Park as a language-grounding candidate; PRISM-VLA makes this high risk. |
| Semi-Supervised VLA, 2026-06 | https://arxiv.org/html/2606.21493v1 | Pushes VLA learning with partially labeled data. | First evidence likely needs heavy model training and broad data. | Park: attractive but not a 24-48 hour first executable experiment. |
| ActionMap, 2026-06 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06904 and https://github.com/showlab/ActionMap | Replaces single-point action decoders with voxel heatmap action heads and reports gains over L1 heads. | Local mini-anchor failed mean-action, MLP, and candidate-collapse gates; full reproduction is out of scope. | Do not continue ActionMap code now. Use only as literature context for action-representation topics. |
| Spline Policy, 2026-06 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07386 | Replaces fixed action chunks with spline parameters, exposing temporal/geometric structure, resampling, uncertainty, and controller compatibility. | VLA-specific controller-valid execution under contact, long-horizon replay, and simple timing/retarget baselines remain key questions. | Strong candidate: constraint-validated spline action interface, but only after baseline-first evidence design. |
| Let It Be Simple, 2026-06 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05737 | Shows one-step VLA action generation can match multi-step diffusion with high-noise biased training. | Leaves little novelty for latency-only one-step sampling; strong high-noise schedule baseline is mandatory. | Reject latency-only topic. |
| PHASER, 2026-06 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03598 | Phase-aware semantic replay for continual VLA learning, with gains across multiple VLA backbones. | New replay-memory topics must beat phase-aware replay, uniform replay, nearest-demo, and no-replay controls. | Park: too close to latest paper unless a distinct failure mode is found. |
| TTT-VLA, 2026-06 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03127 | Test-time latent prompt optimization for deployment shifts. | Requires interaction data and must beat no-update, safety-only, and simple calibration. | Park: promising but first experiment may drift into implementation-heavy adaptation. |
| VLA-JEPA, 2026-02 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10098 | Leakage-free latent state prediction for robust VLA pretraining, evaluated on LIBERO, LIBERO-Plus, SimplerEnv, and real tasks. | Heavy pretraining makes it poor for a 24-48 hour first gate. | Use as background, not immediate topic. |
| OpenVLA-OFT, 2025 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19645 | Optimized fine-tuning recipe with strong LIBERO success and faster action generation. | Full fine-tuning is outside current safe scope. | Baseline/reference only. |
| GR00T N1 and N1.6, 2025-2026 | https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14734 and https://research.nvidia.com/labs/gear/gr00t-n1_6/ | Open humanoid VLA foundation model family with improved N1.6 results. | Access, hardware, and benchmark scope make first local evidence risky. | Anchor for future multi-model claims, not first executable gate. |
| pi0 and pi0.5, 2024-2025 | https://arxiv.org/html/2410.24164v1 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.16054 | Flow-based general robot control and open-world VLA generalization. | Heavy model access and data scale make first local evidence hard. | Anchor for broad claims only after a smaller topic survives. |
