# Epoch 7 contact-transition Stage 0B adjudication

Decision: `STAGE0_TRIVIAL_EQUIVALENCE`

The valid 7,888-row, three-seed probe did not establish task-held-out visual
predictability beyond causal nonvisual history. Median visual any-transition
AUPRC was 0.0674 versus 0.1016 for the same-width nonvisual control and 0.0567
prevalence. The visual-to-prevalence ratio was 1.19 rather than the required
2.0; visual-minus-nonvisual AUPRC was -0.0342 rather than +0.10. Supported
typed-bin macro AP was also 0.0118 below the nonvisual control, and visual won
on only one of four validation tasks rather than three.

The privileged ridge diagnostic independently found no typed action headroom.
On 627 held-out boundary rows, the tune-selected binary-contact control had
0.244320 NRMSE, while adding typed topology produced 0.244540 NRMSE: a -0.09%
relative reduction. Only two of four tasks improved, and because aggregate
gain was nonpositive, the shuffled-gain fraction was undefined and failed by
contract. All generated predictions were finite.

The result does not deny that typed non-robot contact transitions occur; Stage
0A established that they do. It shows that this exact training-supervision
formulation is equivalent or weaker than causal nonvisual/binary controls on
the frozen headroom tests. No method, VLA training, policy rollout, or paper is
authorized. The exact Candidate K route is closed and may not be weakened or
rescued within Epoch 7.
